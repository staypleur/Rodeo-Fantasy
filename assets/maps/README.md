# Cafe review assets

Unpublished layout draft. Final visual approval and a configured Cafe Place are pending.

- `SpaceCafeReview.rbxmx`: static layout draft following the owner's latest top-view reference. Eleven main islands, twelve bridges and two circular riding gardens (2,040 stud diameter each) replace the earlier rectangular garden. It contains no gameplay scripts. Palace and pavilion architecture remains a blockout.
- `CafeCuratedFurniture.rbxmx`: nine selected furniture templates from Roblox's [House Furniture Pack – Duvall Drive](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/10847897579/House-Furniture-Pack-Duvall-Drive). Only geometry, seats and surface appearances are retained. The original pack's 101 script objects were removed before selection; the complete pack is not distributed here.
- `CafeTablePlant.rbxmx`: selected geometry from [Aesthetic Potted Table Plant](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/12032209888/Aesthetic-Potted-Table-Plant), creator `reggiedi3d`. Creator Store reports 2,132 triangles and one MeshPart; the model also contains pot geometry. Used four times near the sofas.
- `CafeSky.rbxmx`: [Sombrero Galaxy Sky](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/10725421130/Sombrero-Galaxy-Sky), creator `FrozenIIElsa12`.
- `CafeFurniture.rbxmx` / `CafeFurnitureOrigin.txt`: initial small templates from `brandonlouvetmich2`: [Classic Sofa](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/16099816431/Classic-Sofa), [Wood Shelf](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/16184405635/Wood-Shelf). Kept as authoring inputs; final placement replaces them with the selected Roblox furniture.

These were acquired as free Creator Store items through the user's signed-in account. Attribution records the source; it is not a claim that marketplace availability grants unrestricted rights outside Roblox.

- `CafeSakura.rbxmx`: selected trunk and leaf geometry from [Sakura Tree – Detailed Cherry Blossom Low Poly](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/105144995503983/Sakura-Tree-Detailed-Cherry-Blossom-Low-Poly), creator `Ace_Code645039`. Two source script objects and the thumbnail camera were removed. Forty placed trees replace primitive tree placeholders; dark-side leaves are tinted purple. Transparent foliage density is deliberately limited.
- `CafeNatureCurated.rbxmx`: four selected templates from Roblox's [Forest Pack](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/6432306802): RhododendronVar01, MediumMossBoulder01, SmallRock01 and WildFlower03. Five MeshParts and five SurfaceAppearances are retained. Thirteen scripts were removed from the source pack before selection. Cached TexturePack properties were removed, while the original texture-map references were retained. The full pack is not included.
- `CafeScenicCurated.rbxmx`: geometry-only selections from [Gazebo](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/4079425387/Gazebo), `RobotGeometry`; [fountain](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/8428072500/fountain), `7777max`; [Park Bench](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/5708639035/Park-Bench), `mildgamespro`; [Classic Cafeteria Kit](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/14318178739/Classic-Cafeteria-Kit), `IKEAPP` (coffee, plate and waffle only); and [Coffee Machine](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/5219913669/Coffee-Machine), `Lunatic_Pineapple`. Two coffee-machine scripts, all source sounds, lights, dispensers and other behavior are excluded. Gazebo/fountain colors match the two island themes. Benches and new food/machine props are decorative; the existing sofa Seats remain usable. `tools/extract_cafe_extras.py` extracts these choices from the explicitly saved source review.

Run `python tools/build_cafe_review.py` to generate `dist/ReviewModels/CafePlanet-Inspection.rbxlx`. `tools/cafe_island_layout.py` arranges the islands, keeps the curated cafe furniture, and opens perimeter guards at bridge entrances. `tools/cafe_landscape.py` adds four outdoor seating terraces, six garden retreats, planted ribbons and cafe/palace forecourt fountains. The giant-garden centre and bridge approaches remain open. Decorations are anchored, have no touch/query handling or shadows, and use no new lights or effects; terrace floors and usable seats retain their necessary interaction. Island-centre distances are 1.4 times the initial reference-layout draft; island sizes and bridge widths are unchanged. Earlier review places are superseded. The review is unpublished, and the build does not change the main lobby or hunting place. All islands and bridges are included together under Workspace.RodeoCafe in this one inspection place. Review characters temporarily walk at 180 studs/second on initial spawn and respawn. In CafeReviewCapacity, set INSPECTION_MODE=false to restore 40. This temporary override is not applied to the main game configuration.

Distance streaming is enabled in the review place. The furniture's PBR textures, triangle totals, actual mobile memory, 20-player networking and 20 giant pets remain unmeasured. The garden's size is a layout proposal, not a performance guarantee.

The themed review initially used a white central monster court, furnished pink/dark indoor cafes on the two small cafe islands, and sports-field markings inside the two giant riding gardens. `tools/cafe_palette.py` tints existing geometry and SurfaceAppearance colors without adding texture maps. Source mesh/map references are reused. The court and fields are visual layouts only; no battle or sports rules are implemented. Eight prototype coffee-machine parts are replaced by the already curated free machine source. Architecture is still a blockout, not final reference-quality scenery.

Latest owner revision removes the central court and replaces it with a 240-stud floating planet and static orbit. `CafePlanet.rbxmx` contains one MeshPart and one SurfaceAppearance from [earth mesh](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/16325622452/earth-mesh), creator PenguinsStudio_PS; the store reports 3,072 triangles. It is imported through Studio GetObjects, stripped of UniqueId/cached TexturePack data, and contains no scripts. The unchanged original surface maps are retained. Generic InsertService LoadAsset did not have access; Studio model import succeeded.

Dining tables rotate to match their chair rows; every dining chair and indoor/outdoor sofa faces its table. `tools/cafe_collision.py` restores furniture, rock, bench, gazebo and fountain collision, adds 58 simple trunk cylinders, and keeps foliage/flowers/tiny display props passable. Furniture uses box collisions, rocks hulls, gazebo and fountain unions default fidelity to retain openings. Source collision changes require mobile physics profiling; they are not cost free.

Building candidates were actually inspected in Studio but excluded from the final map: [Gothic Castle](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/85986263900466/Gothic-Castle), Sergeant_Roach (3,804 descendants including 56 lights); [Cafe Building](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/32187989/Cafe-Building), JungleManiac (1,469 descendants). Importing entire dense buildings would conflict with the current mobile/20-giant-pet budget. Existing architecture remains a blockout; these candidates are not claimed as installed finished buildings. Their source behavior was never executed.
